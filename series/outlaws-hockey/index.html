Austin Outlaws Hockey HQ

Macy's brand of big, protective badass marshmallows comes to pro hockey! A brand-new franchise full of alpha males about to be slammed into the boards by the strong, sassy women who prove to be the best draft picks these players have ever faced.

Meet the Outlaws: GRIZZ • BRANT • MICHAEL

Welcome to Austin, Texas, where cold ice sparks hot romance!

The men of the Austin Outlaws expansion team have spent their careers chasing wins and protecting their teammates at all costs. Falling hard and fast for the women who skate into their lives? That's the one hit to the chest none of them see coming.

Every book features protective badass-marshmallow heroes, strong and sassy heroines, fun and witty banter, heartfelt emotional journeys, and earned HEAs. You'll meet current players, former players, and the men and women who keep the Outlaws running behind the scenes.

Austin Outlaws Hockey—a fresh, low-angst, high-fun take on sports romance.
